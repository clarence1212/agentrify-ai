Help us finalise Agentrify.

We are inviting a small number of organisations to work with us before release, shaping the platform around real agents, real data and real governance requirements.

Track A

For leaders

For executive and operations teams with agents in use or planned, who want them measured and governed properly.

  • A value and governance review of one or two agents
  • Early access to Value and approval workflows
  • Direct input into reporting for boards and regulators
Track B

For developers

For engineering and AI platform teams building multi-agent systems who want rigorous testing and safe improvement.

  • Early access to Studio, Evals and Evolve
  • Working sessions with our product team
  • Influence over integrations and the roadmap

Four steps.

  1. ApplyTell us about your organisation and the agents you have in mind.
  2. Scoping callWe agree a use case, success measures and data arrangements.
  3. Build togetherA time-boxed engagement on one agent, with regular reviews.
  4. Shape the releaseYour feedback directly informs what we launch.
