Use cases

Where governed agents add value.

Examples of agents Agentrify is designed to build and improve, what each would be measured against, and where people stay in charge.

Banking and financial services

Example agents
Credit memo preparation, KYC refresh, payment exception handling
Improves against
Cost per case, turnaround, loss rates
Governance focus
MAS expectations; credit decisions above thresholds stay with people

Insurance

Example agents
Claims triage, document extraction, fraud signals
Improves against
Handling cost, cycle time, leakage
Governance focus
Claim denials and vulnerable customers escalated to people

Healthcare administration

Example agents
Coding support, prior authorisation, scheduling
Improves against
Days to be paid, denials, clinician time
Governance focus
No clinical judgement delegated; patient data strictly scoped

Professional services

Example agents
Research, first drafts, due-diligence review
Improves against
Hours per matter, write-offs, margin
Governance focus
Reviewer sign-off; protecting how juniors learn

Supply chain and manufacturing

Example agents
Supplier exceptions, demand signals, quality reports
Improves against
Inventory, scrap, cost per shipment
Governance focus
Safety-critical actions stay with people

Family offices

Example agents
Statement consolidation, capital call processing, reporting
Improves against
Reporting time, errors, adviser cost
Governance focus
Confidentiality; principals approve every material action

Illustrative examples, not client case studies.
